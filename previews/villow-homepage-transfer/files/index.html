There’s more than one way to make Villow yours.

With the Villow extension

The Shorts fan

Loves scrolling through Shorts and still wants time for longer videos.

  • Limits Shorts scrolling to 15 minutes a day.
  • Sets a 45-minute daily limit for all of YouTube.

With the Villow extension

The curious browser

Likes discovering videos YouTube picks for him, without Shorts or an endless feed.

  • His home feed ends once he’s seen 100 recommendations that day.
  • Turns off Shorts on YouTube.

With the Villow app

The channel gardener

Builds his subscriptions feed around the videos he actually wants to watch.

  • Chooses short or long videos, channel by channel.
  • Uses title filters and playback speeds that suit each channel.

With the Villow app

The evening planner

Keeps weekdays light on YouTube, with more time to watch at the weekend.

  • Chooses different limits for workdays and weekends.
  • Schedules time away and a reminder for bed.

With the Villow app + extension

The all-rounder

Moves between YouTube and Villow depending on what she feels like watching.

  • Keeps her viewing progress in sync across devices.
  • Counts time in the extension and Villow app towards one daily limit.
